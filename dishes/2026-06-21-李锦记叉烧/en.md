## Method

### Marinate

Cut the pork into long strips about **3–4cm** thick. Mix char siu sauce, soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, garlic, and five-spice, then coat the pork.

Refrigerate for at least **4 hours**, ideally overnight. Turn once if possible.

### Roast

Preheat the fan oven to **190°C**. Line a tray with foil to catch drips and place the pork on a rack; if you have no rack, roast directly on the tray and turn halfway.

Scrape off excess marinade. Roast for **18–22 minutes**. Turn, brush with marinade or char siu sauce, and roast for another **15–20 minutes**.

### Honey glaze

Mix honey with a little char siu sauce and hot water. Brush over the pork. Increase to **220°C fan** or grill mode and cook for **3–6 minutes** until caramelised at the edges.

Rest for **8–10 minutes** before slicing.

---

## Key Tips

- Pork shoulder, collar, or skinless belly work best; lean loin dries out easily
- Scrape off thick marinade before roasting or it will burn before the meat colours
- Watch closely during the final glaze because the sauce and honey scorch quickly
- Rest before slicing to keep the meat juicy
