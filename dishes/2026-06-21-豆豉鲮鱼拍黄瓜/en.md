## Method

### Smash the cucumber

Smash the cucumber and cut into chunks. If it releases a lot of water, toss with a small pinch of salt, rest for 5 minutes, then drain.

### Mix the dace

Use half a can of fried dace. Crush the fish and use only a little of the canned oil. Mix with garlic, vinegar, chilli oil, and sugar.

### Toss

Pour the dace mixture over the cucumber, toss well, and rest for **5–10 minutes** before serving.

---

## Key Tips

- The canned fish is already salty, so extra salt or soy sauce is usually unnecessary
- Do not add all the canned oil or it will be greasy
- Cucumber balances the saltiness and richness of the dace
- The same topping also works with starch jelly, noodles, or congee
