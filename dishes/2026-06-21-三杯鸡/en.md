## Method

### Brown the chicken

Cut chicken thigh into 4–5cm chunks and pat dry. Heat sesame oil over medium-low heat, fry ginger until the edges curl, then add garlic and chilli.

Turn to medium heat, add the chicken, and let it brown before tossing. Build a little caramelised surface before adding sauce.

### Braise

Add three-cup chicken sauce and rice wine. If the pan is dry, add 2–4 tablespoons water. Cover and simmer over medium-low heat for **8–10 minutes**.

### Reduce and add basil

Uncover and reduce until the sauce is thick and clings to the chicken. Add basil right at the end, toss just until wilted, and serve.

---

## Key Tips

- Do not burn sesame oil; it turns bitter
- Brown the chicken before saucing for better flavour
- Reduce the sauce until glossy; three-cup chicken should not be soupy
- Add basil at the end so the aroma stays fresh
