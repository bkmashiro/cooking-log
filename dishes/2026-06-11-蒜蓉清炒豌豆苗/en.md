## Method

### Prepare

Wash the pea shoots and dry them as well as possible. If the stems are long, cut them in half. Mince or smash the garlic.

### Quick stir-fry

Heat the pan until clearly hot, then add the oil. Add the garlic and fry just until fragrant; do not let it brown deeply.

Add the pea shoots immediately and stir-fry over high heat for **30–60 seconds**. Once they just wilt and turn glossy, season with salt, sugar, and white pepper. Toss quickly and serve at once.

---

## Key Tips

- Dry the pea shoots well or they will release too much water
- Use high heat and a short cooking time; overcooking makes them watery, tough, and dull
- Add salt at the end to reduce water loss
- Do not burn the garlic; bitter garlic will overpower the sweetness of the pea shoots
