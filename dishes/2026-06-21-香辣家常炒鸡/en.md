## Method

### Brown the chicken

Chop chicken thigh into small pieces. Soak for 10 minutes to remove blood, then drain and pat dry. Heat oil in a hot pan and fry the chicken over medium-high heat for **5–8 minutes** until moisture cooks off and the surface lightly browns.

### Aromatics and seasoning

Add ginger, garlic, dried chillies, and Sichuan peppercorns if using. Fry until fragrant. Add light soy, dark soy, Shaoxing wine, oyster sauce, and sugar; toss until the chicken is coloured.

### Simmer and reduce

Add beer or hot water. Cover and simmer over medium-low heat for **15–20 minutes**. Uncover and reduce until the sauce thickens, then add green chillies and stir-fry for **1–2 minutes**.

---

## Key Tips

- Skip blanching; soaking, drying, and browning gives better flavour
- Add green chillies at the end for colour and aroma
- A little doubanjiang or hotpot base makes it spicier but more Sichuan-style
- Reduce until the sauce clings to the chicken, not soupy
