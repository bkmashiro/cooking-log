## Method

### Make the slurry

Mix tapioca starch with 150ml cold water until no dry starch remains.

### Gelatinise

Bring the remaining water and brown sugar to a boil. Reduce to medium-low and pour in the tapioca slurry while stirring constantly. Keep stirring until it turns from cloudy to translucent and very thick.

Cook and stir for another **2–3 minutes** to fully cook the starch.

### Set and serve

Pour into a container and tap gently to release large bubbles. Cool, then refrigerate for at least **2 hours**. Cut into cubes and serve with milk tea, coconut milk, milk, or brown sugar syrup.

---

## Key Tips

- Tapioca paste is very sticky and can trap bubbles; this is normal
- Stir slowly with a spatula instead of whisking hard
- Wet the knife before cutting
- This is not traditional starch jelly; it is closer to a chewy crystal cake
