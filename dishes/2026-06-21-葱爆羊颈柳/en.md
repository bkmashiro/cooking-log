## Method

### Slice and marinate

Slice lamb neck fillet against the grain into **2–3mm** pieces. Chill it in the freezer for 15–20 minutes if it is too soft to slice. Marinate with 1 tablespoon soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, white pepper, and starch. Finish with a little oil and rest for **10–15 minutes**.

### Flash-fry the lamb

Heat the pan well, add oil, and spread the lamb out quickly. Stir-fry over medium-high heat just until it changes colour, about **30–60 seconds**, then remove.

### Scallions and finish

Add more oil if needed. Fry ginger and scallions over medium-high heat until the scallion edges char slightly and smell sweet. Return the lamb and add a sauce of 1 tablespoon soy sauce, oyster sauce, a little sugar, and 1 tablespoon water. Toss for **20–40 seconds**.

Add cumin and a few drops of vinegar around the edge of the pan, then serve.

---

## Key Tips

- Slice lamb neck fillet thinly against the grain
- The first fry should only colour the lamb; it will cook again at the end
- Use plenty of scallion and let the edges char slightly
- Mix the sauce before cooking because the final stir-fry is very fast
